# Privacy Policy — Tiny Dungeon Survivor **Last updated:** October 11, 2026 ## Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how Tiny Dungeon Survivor ("the Game") handles information when you play it. ## Information Collection and Use Tiny Dungeon Survivor is designed to be playable without creating an account or providing personal information. The Game stores gameplay progress, such as coins and permanent upgrades, locally on your device. This information is used to save your progress and is not transmitted to the developer by the Game. The current version of the Game does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information. ## Third-Party Services The current version of the Game does not use advertising, analytics, or other third-party services to collect user information. If third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe their relevant data practices. ## Data Storage and Deletion Gameplay save data is stored locally on your device. You can generally remove this data by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game. The exact behavior may depend on your device and Android backup settings. Because the current version does not transmit gameplay save data to the developer, the developer does not maintain a separate server-side copy of that save data. ## Children's Privacy The Game is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been collected through the Game, please contact the developer. ## Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this URL. ## Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: Developer: Tiny Dungeon Survivor Email: akdevgame@gmail.com
